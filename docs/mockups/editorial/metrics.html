Model evaluation

Duplicate detection · gold labels

Two encoders are tied. The cheaper one runs on this laptop.

Average precision for finding true duplicates among 8,646 candidate pairs, scored against 150 hand-labelled gold pairs. A local 384-dimension MiniLM and Amazon Titan Embed v2 on Bedrock are statistically indistinguishable at this sample size — their 95% intervals overlap almost entirely — and both clear the lexical baselines by a wide margin. That is the finding: the managed 1,024-dimension model buys nothing here, so the loop keeps the local one and spends the Bedrock budget elsewhere.
