A 384-dimension local encoder matches Amazon Titan v2 on this corpus.

Average precision for the duplicate class over 150 human-labelled pairs drawn from 132 issues and PRs, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 1,000 replicates. The two dense encoders are statistically indistinguishable; both clear BM25 by a wide margin. That is the whole argument for keeping inference local and paying Bedrock for nothing.
